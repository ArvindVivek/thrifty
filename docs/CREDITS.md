## Removed in the web release (2026-09-29)

The hackathon build used art Kitchen Labs has no licence to ship. All of it was deleted:
- video game weapon and shield images (Riot Games' Valorant), and the weapon names;
- the Cloud9 logo on the catcher;
- Kodee, JetBrains' Kotlin mascot (15 poses).

The hand-drawn power-up SVGs from the hackathon were replaced by Lucide icons for one consistent
icon set.
